GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful
details.

Does app blocking work on every Android device in exactly the same way?

No. Android versions, device manufacturers, permissions and background limits differ. The app checks important permissions and provides a setup guide, but cannot promise identical enforcement on every device.

Does the companion need an internet connection to animate?

The 3D companion scene is rendered locally. Spoken voice depends on an offline text-to-speech voice installed on the device. If no suitable offline voice is available, visual reactions and sound effects can still work.

Can I reduce movement or turn off sound?

Yes. Use the companion sound control and the Reduce Motion setting to make the experience quieter and less animated.

What does the progress tracker record?

It uses entries and check-ins that you choose to save. It does not try to infer private behaviour from sensors or monitor your personal activity.
